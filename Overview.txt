UX FLOW

Visit Homepage
Click on Create New Account and move to /signup

Click on Continue with Google and chose an account

Click on Get a Copy of Template and move to spreadsheet

On Spreadsheet, click on GoodSheetLife > Connect to GoodSheetLife, opening up modal of iframe /signup?sheet_id={spreadsheet_id}&script_id={appsscript_id}&email={user_email}

On /signup, check if user exists in supabase via email

if user exists, create app in supabase and redirect to /mypage
